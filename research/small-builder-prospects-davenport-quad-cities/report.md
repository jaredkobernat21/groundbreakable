# Small Builder/Developer Prospects — Davenport, IA / Quad Cities (Scott County)

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Method:** `research/small-builder-lead-sourcing-method.md`, applied to a new market for the first time (first run was KC metro).
**Prepared as:** sales-prospecting research, not a finished pitch — phone numbers are business-published, not skip-traced.

---

## 0. Method summary

**What worked:** BuildZoom per-city listings (`buildzoom.com/<city>-ia/home-builders`) again surfaced permit-count data directly, for Davenport, Bettendorf, Eldridge, LeClaire, Walcott, Princeton, Blue Grass, Long Grove, and (secondarily) Muscatine. BBB profiles were the most reliable second-source corroboration — where a BBB profile existed and matched BuildZoom's owner name, address, and phone independently, that was strong enough for Medium-High confidence.

**What didn't work / was different from KC:** Iowa has no general contractor license — only a Division of Labor revenue-based registration (required above $2,000/yr). BuildZoom nonetheless displays an Iowa "contractor license" status for each profile, and it was "inactive/expired" for the large majority of candidates checked here, including some clearly real, active, well-reviewed businesses — this status appears far less meaningful in Iowa than the Johnson County, KS license status was, and was **not treated as a disqualifying signal** on its own. No BuildZoom-shared-call-tracking-number pattern (the KC gotcha) was observed here — every phone number pulled from a distinct BuildZoom profile in this market was structurally distinct, not a shared base number with different extensions — but that doesn't replace independent corroboration, which was still required.

**The bigger, more consequential gap:** three candidates that looked like strong "few permits" fits on BuildZoom (Jared Kerkhoff Homes — 3 permits; Diamond Builders of Davenport — technically 20; Mike Stych Builders — 4 permits) turned out, once searched independently, to be established businesses: founded 2000, 1999, and 1993 respectively, one with 11-50 employees and industry awards. BuildZoom's permit history appears to be a recent-years sample, not a full career count — a "few permits" reading here is a much weaker signal of "few builds" than it was in the KC run, and every candidate needs an explicit years-in-business check, not just a permit count, before being treated as early-stage. (This lesson has been folded back into `research/small-builder-lead-sourcing-method.md`.)

The Quad Cities Home Builders Association is branded **Quad Cities Builders & Remodelers Association (QCBR)**, qcbr.org — its live member-directory search wasn't scriptable in the time available (same pattern as KC's HBA), so only members who separately surfaced in search results were checked.

## 2. Deep dives

### 1 — Faulhaber Custom Homes (Tom Faulhaber), Le Claire, IA

**What Groundbreakable would offer him:** A small, multi-decade family operation (father + sons) still working at low volume (~5 tracked permits) — likely land-constrained rather than staffed for site-scouting. The pitch: he's spending his own time finding lots when a site-and-buildability screen could do that work for him.
**Confidence: Medium-High.** Phone number and owner name are corroborated identically across BuildZoom and BBB. **Unresolved discrepancy, flag before contact:** BuildZoom lists his business address as 23347 277th Ave, Le Claire, while BBB lists 1302 Woodland Ln, Le Claire — two different addresses for what appears to be the same phone/owner. Two lower-quality aggregators (Yelp, Allbiz) also showed two additional, different phone numbers ((563) 332-6063 and (563) 340-6004) that were NOT used — only the BuildZoom+BBB-corroborated number is reported here.
**What needs to be verified before calling:** which address is current (could be a home vs. a second property); confirm the number reaches Tom directly.

### 2 — Core Designs LLC (Ryan Carstensen), Le Claire, IA

**What Groundbreakable would offer him:** A solo, architecture-trained custom builder serving both the Iowa and Illinois sides of the Quad Cities, explicitly positioning on personalization and transparency rather than volume — exactly the profile of someone without a land-acquisition team. Buildability screening (utilities, flood, access) before committing to a lot fits his "simplicity and transparency" brand directly.
**Confidence: Medium-High.** Owner name, phone, and address match exactly across BuildZoom and BBB (A+ rating, file opened 2021). One minor gap: a separate source described the company as "founded in 2008," which doesn't cleanly match the 2021 BBB file date — likely reflects Ryan's personal career start vs. this specific LLC's formal registration, not a red flag, but worth a casual confirmation.
**What needs to be verified before calling:** current Iowa registration/license status (BuildZoom shows expired, but Iowa's registration requirement works differently than a KS-style license — don't assume this disqualifies him).

## 5. Guardrails followed

- No people-search/data-broker/skip-trace sites used for any phone number.
- No BuildZoom-listed number used without at least attempting independent corroboration; the two shortlisted candidates' numbers matched a second source exactly.
- No license-portal solicitation-certification gate was encountered this run.
- Confidence levels held to the same bar as KC — "Medium-High" means corroborated across two independent public sources, not verified against a government parcel/ownership record.
