Development Intelligence

Development intelligence starts before the property search.

Groundbreakable runs on two disciplines: Momentum, which identifies where development is heading, and Buildability, which determines what can be built once we're watching.

Momentum · Where

Connecting the early signals of development.

A single signal rarely means much on its own. A rezoning here, a utility extension there — alone, each is just an event. Groundbreakable looks for independent signals converging on the same geography: evidence that development conditions in an area are beginning to change.

Planning Infrastructure Utilities Land Use Development Activity Investment
Development Momentum

We don't claim to predict development with certainty. We describe areas as emerging, gaining momentum, or worth watching — based on evidence that conditions are changing, not on guarantees about what happens next.

Buildability · What

Momentum identifies where. Buildability reveals what.

Once an area is worth watching, we analyze what it can actually support — the zoning, infrastructure, and site conditions that turn momentum into a real opportunity.
