Founder

Jared Kobernat is the founder of Groundbreakable.

He builds and operates Groundbreakable directly — the data, the scoring, and the judgment behind every signal — after years of watching development decisions get made in places most people never think to look.

Jared Kobernat, founder of Groundbreakable and ROQ.

Before Groundbreakable.

Jared spent his early career in sales leadership — learning firsthand how businesses actually win and lose customers, and how much comes down to who has better information going in. He later taught himself to build software: websites, native apps, CRMs, and the internal tools that now run ROQ, the company behind Groundbreakable.

That background is why Groundbreakable isn't built as another public-records database to search. Jared builds and maintains the data pipelines, scoring models, and mapping tools himself, market by market — connecting permits, zoning, and infrastructure spending into the kind of signal a developer can actually act on.
